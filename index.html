The Memory Wall

The three companies that manufacture high bandwidth memory, and the research that found the wall. Specifications below were read from each company's own product page on 11 November 2026.

The three companies that make the memory

SK hynix
HBM4, HBM3E, HBM3, HBM2E

HBM4 listed at 24 to 48 GB, 11.0 Gbps and higher, 2.8 TB/s and higher, in 8, 12 and 16 high stacks.

product.skhynix.com/products/dram/hbm.go
Samsung
HBM4E, HBM4, HBM3E, HBM3

HBM4 at 36 GB, 12 high, up to 13.0 Gbps per pin, up to 3,300 GB/s, 2048 I/O. HBM4E up to 64 GB, up to 16 high, up to 16.0 Gbps, up to 4 TB/s per stack, built on what Samsung calls the industry's first 1c DRAM with a 4 nm logic base die.

semiconductor.samsung.com/dram/hbm
Micron
HBM4, HBM3E

HBM4 36GB 12H in high volume production, over 11 Gb/s per pin, more than 2.8 TB/s per stack, stated at 2.3 times the bandwidth of HBM3E with over 20 percent better power efficiency. 1024 bit interface, 32 independent channels, 1-beta DRAM node.

micron.com/products/memory/hbm

The research

Five Ways NVIDIA Co-Design Maximizes the Value of Every Byte in the AI Factory
NVIDIA, 5 November 2026

NVIDIA's own engineers call HBM "the fastest, most expensive, and most contested memory tier."

developer.nvidia.com/blog/five-ways-nvidia-co-design-maximizes-the-value-of-every-byte-in-the-ai-factory
FlashOffloading: Low-Latency KV Cache Offloading for Long-Context LLM Serving
arXiv 2611.07542, 5 November 2026. Huawei Technologies, Huawei Ascend Computing Technology Development, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

The paper that prices the wall and then routes around it.

arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07542
Resource-Efficient Reinforcement Learning Post-Training through Elastic Expert Parallelism
arXiv 2611.06984, 4 November 2026. Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University

The training side of the same constraint: workers finished but unable to release what they hold.

arxiv.org/abs/2611.06984
BlockLock: Reproducible LLM Inference with Fixed Batch Geometry
arXiv 2611.06977, 4 November 2026

Not in the video. Included because it shows even reproducibility now turns on memory geometry.

arxiv.org/abs/2611.06977
